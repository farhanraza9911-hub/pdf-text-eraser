Privacy Policy: PDF Text Eraser & Editor

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Developer: Farhan Bashir (Bashir Sons of Apps)

Contact: farhanraza9911@gmail.com

App: PDF Text Eraser & Editor, Android package com.farhan.pdfeditor, and its web version

This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • Your PDFs, pictures and the text in them stay on your device. We do not upload, read, store or sell them.
  • We (the developer) do not collect any personal data. The app has no accounts and no login.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID and other information to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Purchases ("Remove ads") are processed by Google Play.

2. Files you open and edit

  • PDFs and pictures you open are copied to the app's private storage on your device, only so they can be edited. Temporary copies are deleted automatically.
  • Editing, text deletion, text recognition (OCR), merging and saving all happen on your device, offline.
  • Your original file is never changed. Edited files are saved where you choose.
  • The app never sends document or picture contents to us or to any server.

3. Camera and photos

  • "Take a picture" opens your phone's own camera app. The app does not request the camera permission.
  • Photos you take or choose are processed on your device only.

4. Information we collect

None. We do not run servers that receive your data. We do not use analytics or tracking tools.

The app keeps a few settings on your device only: recently opened file names, theme, default save folder, and whether ads were removed. You can clear the recent list in Settings → "Clear recent history". Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

5. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

The app shows interstitial ads after saving, exporting, merging or sharing. To provide ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share:

  • the device advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language),
  • ad interactions (views and clicks), and diagnostics.

This data is collected and processed by Google under Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): where the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads. You can change your choice later in Settings → "Ad privacy choices".

Your control: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Google Play Billing (Remove ads)

Buying "Remove ads" ($5, one time) is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase exists, so that it can turn off ads and restore the purchase on a new device.

6. Ads-remove keys

Keys are checked offline on your device. To lock a key to one phone, the app shows an anonymous device ID (Android ID). This ID is only shown to you to copy and send yourself; the app never transmits it.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through ads, contact us and we will help you contact Google.

9. Security and data retention

Document processing stays on your device and inside the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Temporary files are deleted after use. We keep no data on servers. Advertising data is kept by Google according to its policies.

10. Web version

The web version runs entirely in your browser. Files you open are not uploaded; it works offline after it is loaded. It does not show ads and does not use cookies for tracking.
